# Leave

## Parental leave
All employees get 26 weeks of paid parental leave, usable within 18 months of the birth or adoption.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year.

# Expenses

## Travel
Book travel through the travel portal. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.

## Equipment
Each employee has a home-office budget of 500 EUR every two years.
