## Steps

1. **List the AI changes** (10 min) from Labs 4–11 and any others in flight.
2. **Pick metrics** (25 min). For each of the top 3 changes: one outcome, one quality and one experience metric, each defined precisely ([choosing metrics](/ai-native-teams/m12-value/metrics/)).
3. **Pull the baseline** (25 min). Get historical numbers where they exist. Where they don't, plan a 2–4 week measurement period.
4. **Choose the comparison method** (15 min) from [the table](/ai-native-teams/m12-value/measuring/#ways-to-compare-weakest-to-strongest). Is a comparison group or staggered rollout possible?
5. **Draft the value-update template** (15 min) using [the one-page structure](/ai-native-teams/m12-value/roi/#telling-the-value-story) and set the date for the first one.

## Deliverable

A **measurement plan**: metrics with definitions, baselines, data sources, comparison method, owners, and the date of the first value update.
