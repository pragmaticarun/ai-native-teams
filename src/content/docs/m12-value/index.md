"Are we getting value from AI?" is the question every sponsor eventually asks. Teams that can answer it with evidence keep their investment and their credibility. Teams that answer with anecdotes, or with vanity metrics like "lines of code generated", lose both.

**Time:** ~2 hours · **For:** leads, AI owners and anyone who owns a workflow

## Learning goals

- Choose outcome metrics that matter and avoid vanity and perverse metrics.
- Establish a baseline before changing a workflow.
- Run comparisons that support a conclusion: before/after with controls, pilots, and simple experiments.
- Combine quantitative and qualitative evidence into an honest value story and an ROI estimate.
