Good AI governance makes it **easy to do the right thing quickly**. Bad governance either blocks everything (so people use unapproved tools in secret) or approves everything (so risks pile up). This module gives teams enough to work with legal, security and compliance as partners.

:::note[Not legal advice]
This module summarises public sources to help teams ask the right questions. Always confirm obligations with your legal and compliance teams.
:::

**Time:** ~2.5 hours · **For:** everyone (lessons 3–4); leads, owners of AI systems, and anyone in regulated work (all)

## Learning goals

- Summarise the EU AI Act's risk-based structure and its timeline as amended in 2026.
- Know the main voluntary frameworks: NIST AI RMF, its Generative AI Profile, and ISO/IEC 42001.
- Write a practical team AI use policy.
- Evaluate AI vendors and decide which data can go into which tools.
- Run a lightweight AI system register and review process.
