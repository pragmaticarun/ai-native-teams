<ul class="lab-meta"><li>⏱ 90 min</li><li>👥 Whole team, plus someone from security, legal or compliance if possible</li><li>🧰 The policy template, your organisation's existing policies</li></ul>

## Goal

Leave with a draft team AI use policy and a first AI register, ready for review by whoever owns governance in your organisation.

## Steps

1. **Collect what exists** (10 min). Organisation-wide AI policy, data classification, approved tool list. Your team policy must fit inside these.
2. **Scenarios** (20 min). In pairs, write 8 realistic "can I…?" questions people on the team actually have. ("Can I paste a customer's error log into the assistant?" "Can I use AI to summarise interview notes?")
3. **Draft** (30 min). Fill in the [policy template](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/policy/#template). Each section owner checks: does the draft answer the scenario questions clearly?
4. **Test** (15 min). Swap drafts between groups. Answer each scenario using only the draft. Mark where the answer was unclear.
5. **Register** (15 min). List every AI assistant, automation or agent the team runs or built in Labs 4–9, with the [register fields](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/vendors-and-data/#the-ai-register).

## Deliverable

A one-page policy draft, the scenario Q&A (great as an FAQ), and the AI register. Send them for review and set a six-month review date.
