"It looked good when I tried it" is how AI features break in production. **Evals** (systematic tests of AI behaviour) are how AI-native teams know whether a prompt, model, tool or workflow is good enough, and whether a change made it better or worse.

**Time:** ~3 hours · **For:** everyone who builds or owns an AI use (lessons 1–2 for everyone)

## Learning goals

- Explain why AI needs evals and how they differ from traditional tests.
- Build an eval set from real examples, with clear pass criteria.
- Use code-based graders, human review and LLM-as-judge appropriately, and calibrate judges against humans.
- Trace and monitor AI systems in production, including cost and latency.
- Run evals as a habit: before launch, on every change, and on a schedule.
