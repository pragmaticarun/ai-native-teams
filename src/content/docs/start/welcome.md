This course takes a **whole team** (not just its engineers) from "we use ChatGPT sometimes" to a team whose everyday work is designed around AI, with the safeguards to trust it.

## Who it is for

| Role | What you will get |
|---|---|
| Engineers | Coding agents, context engineering, RAG, agents and MCP, evals, agent security. |
| Product managers | AI product thinking, prototyping with AI, specs that agents can execute, evals as acceptance criteria. |
| Designers & researchers | AI-assisted research synthesis, prototyping, designing AI features people can trust. |
| Operations, support, sales, marketing, finance, HR | Workflow redesign, automations, knowledge assistants, safe data handling. |
| Team leads & executives | Maturity models, the operating model, governance, change management, measuring value. |

No coding is required for Parts 1, 3 and 4. Part 2 has an engineering-heavy module (M6) and optional technical tracks in M4 and M5; non-engineers can do those labs with no-code tools.

## Structure

Every module has the same shape:

1. **Overview**: why it matters, learning goals, time needed.
2. **Lessons**: concepts, worked examples, common mistakes, and a *Team practice* section.
3. **Lab**: a 60–120 minute hands-on exercise on your team's real work, with a deliverable.
4. **Quiz**: a short self-check, graded in your browser.

## How to run it as a team

The format that works best is a **cohort**: the whole team moves through one module per week.

- **Before the session (60–90 min, alone):** read the lessons, take the quiz.
- **Team session (90–120 min, together):** run the lab, then debrief. Rotate who facilitates.
- **Between sessions:** apply one thing to real work and bring the result (good or bad) to the next session.
- **Keep a shared "AI playbook"** (a wiki page or repo folder). Every lab adds something to it: prompts that work, rules, evals, policies, decisions. By the end, the playbook *is* your AI-native operating manual.

A 13-week cohort at ~3 hours per person per week covers everything. A compressed version for leaders is in [learning paths](/ai-native-teams/start/learning-paths/).

## Ground rules

- **Use real work, safely.** Labs are designed around your actual tasks. Until you finish [M10 · Governance](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/), use only tools your organisation has approved and keep confidential data out of consumer tools.
- **Measure, don't vibe.** "It feels faster" is a hypothesis. M8 and M12 show how to check.
- **Share failures.** The fastest-learning teams make it normal to post "AI got this badly wrong, here's why".
- **Dates matter.** AI tools change monthly. Content was last reviewed in **November 2026**; check the [tools landscape](/ai-native-teams/reference/tools-landscape/) for what changed.
