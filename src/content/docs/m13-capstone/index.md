The capstone turns everything your team produced in the labs into a **90-day AI-native plan** that you present to your sponsor and then execute.

**Time:** 2–3 hours prep + 60 min presentation · **For:** the whole team

## What you'll produce

1. A **current-state summary**: self-assessment (start vs now), maturity placement, key gaps.
2. **Three to five workflow bets** for the next 90 days, each with metrics and a baseline.
3. The **enablers**: context, tools, evals, governance and skills each bet needs.
4. **Operating model**: owners, rituals, champions.
5. **Risks and safeguards.**
6. A **measurement and review plan**.
