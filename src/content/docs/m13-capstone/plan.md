## Step 1 · Where we are (from Labs 1, 11)

- Self-assessment: week 1 vs now, by dimension.
- Placement on one maturity ladder and the DORA capabilities map.
- The three biggest gaps.

## Step 2 · Choose your bets

List candidate workflows (Lab 7 and others). Score each:

| Workflow | Value (1–5) | Feasibility (1–5) | Risk (L/M/H) | Readiness of data & context (1–5) | Pick? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Pick **3–5 bets**: at least one quick win (visible results in 30 days), at least one deeper redesign, and at most one high-risk item, with full safeguards.

## Step 3 · For each bet, fill in a card

```markdown
## Bet: <workflow name>
- Owner: <name> · Champion: <name>
- Today: <current workflow, key pain, baseline metric values>
- Change: <redesigned workflow, AI steps, human checkpoints>
- Tools & context: <approved tools, instructions/skills, knowledge sources, MCP servers>
- Autonomy level & approvals: <e.g. L4 Approver: human approves every outbound email>
- Evals: <eval set, size, threshold>
- Risks & safeguards: <from the threat model; data classes involved>
- Metrics: outcome <…>, quality <…>, experience <…>
- Milestones: day 30 <…>, day 60 <…>, day 90 <…>
- Stop / pivot criteria: <what would make us stop>
```

## Step 4 · Enablers

Across all bets, what must exist?

- **Context:** instructions files, skills, docs to write or clean up.
- **Tools:** access, approvals, connectors.
- **Evals:** sets to build, automation.
- **Governance:** policy sign-off, register entries, reviews needed.
- **Skills:** who needs which modules or pairing.

## Step 5 · Operating model and rhythm (from Lab 11)

Roles and time, rituals in the calendar, the playbook owner.

## Step 6 · Measurement and review (from Lab 12)

Baselines, comparison methods, value updates at day 30, 60 and 90, and a quarterly re-run of the self-assessment.

## Quality check

Before presenting, check the plan against these questions:

- [ ] Does every bet have a baseline and an outcome metric?
- [ ] Does every bet name its human checkpoints and why?
- [ ] Has every AI system been threat-modelled, and is it in the register?
- [ ] Is time protected for the people doing the work?
- [ ] Is there at least one bet that redesigns, not just speeds up, a workflow?
- [ ] Are stop criteria defined?
