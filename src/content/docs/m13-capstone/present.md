## The presentation (30 min + 30 min discussion)

1. **The stance** (2 min): what AI-native means for this team, in one sentence.
2. **Where we are** (5 min): self-assessment movement, gaps, one story from the course.
3. **The bets** (12 min): for each, the before/after workflow, metrics and safeguards. Demo the prototype from Lab 7 if you can.
4. **What it takes** (6 min): enablers, time, budget, decisions needed.
5. **How we'll know** (5 min): metrics, review dates, stop criteria.

Ask for **specific decisions**: approve tool X, protect N hours per week, sign off the policy, sponsor attends the day-30 review.

## Running the 90 days

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Kick-off; confirm baselines; book all rituals |
| Weekly | Show-and-tell (one win, one failure); playbook updates |
| Day 30 | First value update; quick-win results; adjust bets |
| Day 60 | Second value update; decide scale/fix/stop per bet |
| Day 90 | Final update; re-run the self-assessment; plan the next 90 days |

## After the course

- **Keep the cohort rhythm** with a new topic each month (a new tool, a deeper module, a guest from another team).
- **Re-run evals and the tool review quarterly**, because models and tools change.
- **Spread it:** offer to run the course for a neighbouring team, with your playbook as the worked example.
- **Contribute back:** found something wrong or out of date in this course? [Open an issue or PR](https://github.com/pragmaticarun/ai-native-teams).
