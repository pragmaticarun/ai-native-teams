Tools are the easy part. Becoming AI-native is a **change in how people work**, and it needs the same care as any organisational change: a clear direction, roles, rituals, skills, psychological safety, and leaders who model it.

**Time:** ~2.5 hours · **For:** everyone; essential for leads and managers

## Learning goals

- Compare the main published AI maturity models, and use one to set direction without over-trusting it.
- Design a team operating model: roles, champions, rituals and the playbook.
- Lead the human side of change: fears, incentives, psychological safety, and resistance.
- Build skills deliberately, including for early-career people.
