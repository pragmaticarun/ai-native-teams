## Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| **Sponsor** (team lead or exec) | Sets the stance, removes blockers, models usage, protects learning time | Ongoing, visible |
| **AI lead / owner** | Runs the adoption plan, owns the playbook and register, coordinates with security/legal | 20–50% of a role |
| **Champions** (1 per 6–10 people, across functions) | Help peers, collect use cases and failures, test new tools, run demos | ~10% each |
| **Builders** | Build assistants, automations, agents, evals | As needed |
| **Everyone** | Use AI daily, verify output, share learnings, follow the policy | |

Champions work best when they're **respected practitioners**, not the most enthusiastic tool fans, and when the role is recognised in goals and reviews.

## Team and centre

Most organisations end up with a **hub-and-spoke** model:

- **Central (hub):** platform and approved tools, security and legal review, shared infrastructure (enterprise assistant, model gateway, eval tooling), organisation-wide policy and training.
- **Teams (spokes):** workflow redesign, domain-specific prompts, skills, evals and automations, local champions.

Teams know their work; the centre makes it safe and cheap to do. Neither works alone.

## Rituals

| Ritual | Cadence | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **AI show-and-tell** | Weekly or fortnightly, 30 min | Two short demos: one win, one failure, with what was learned |
| **Playbook update** | With each show-and-tell | New prompts, skills, rules or evals get added; stale ones removed |
| **Tool and model review** | Quarterly | Re-run key evals on current models; review tools against needs and cost |
| **AI retro item** | In every retrospective | "Where did AI help or hurt this sprint?" |
| **Register and policy review** | Quarterly / six-monthly | Review the AI register and policy ([M10](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/)) |

## The playbook

The playbook is the team's AI operating manual, built through the labs in this course:

- Which model for what (Lab 1)
- Prompt library (Lab 2)
- Instructions files and skills (Lab 3)
- Knowledge assistant and its eval set (Lab 4)
- Agent cards (Lab 5), repo practices (Lab 6)
- Redesigned workflows (Lab 7), evals (Lab 8), red-team findings (Lab 9)
- Policy and register (Lab 10), roles and rituals (this module's lab)
- Metrics and baseline (Lab 12), 90-day plan (Lab 13)

Keep it where people already work, with owners for each section.
