## The AI-native skill set

| Skill | Why it matters more now |
|---|---|
| **Problem framing and specification** | Agents do what you specify, so unclear thinking now produces wrong work at scale |
| **Context curation and writing** | Instructions, skills, specs and docs are how you direct AI ([M3](/ai-native-teams/m03-context/)) |
| **Critical evaluation** | Knowing what good looks like, and checking efficiently |
| **Workflow thinking** | Seeing the end-to-end process, not just the task ([M7](/ai-native-teams/m07-workflows/)) |
| **Risk judgment** | Matching verification and autonomy to stakes ([M9](/ai-native-teams/m09-safety/)) |
| **Domain expertise** | Still the foundation: you can't evaluate what you don't understand |
| **Tool fluency** | Necessary but quick to learn, and quick to date |

## How people actually learn this

- **On real work**, not in abstract training. This course's labs use your tasks deliberately.
- **With peers:** pairing, show-and-tell, champions.
- **In small daily doses:** "use AI for one new task each day" for a month builds more than a one-day workshop.
- **With feedback:** comparing outputs, reviewing each other's prompts and agent runs.

## Protecting early-career learning

There's a real risk that juniors skip the struggle that builds judgment. Practical guardrails:

- **Explain-back:** juniors explain AI-produced work in their own words before shipping it.
- **Do it first, then compare:** for some learning tasks, attempt alone first, then compare with AI.
- **Use AI as tutor, not just doer:** "explain why this approach", "quiz me on this codebase".
- **Rotate review:** juniors review AI output with a senior, building the evaluation skill explicitly.
- **Keep apprenticeship:** seniors still mentor; AI frees time for it rather than replacing it.

## A minimum training baseline

Under the EU AI Act, organisations must take measures to support AI literacy for staff dealing with AI systems ([M10](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/regulation/)). A reasonable team baseline: everyone completes Start here, M1, M2, M3, M9 and the team policy; builders add M4–M8.
