## Steps

1. **Re-take the self-assessment** (15 min) individually ([self-assessment](/ai-native-teams/start/self-assessment/)). Compare with your week-1 scores. Where did you move? Where not?
2. **Place yourselves on two models** (15 min): one ladder (e.g. MIT CISR stages for the organisation, or Shapiro levels for engineering) and the DORA capabilities map. Which capability is weakest?
3. **Roles** (15 min). Name the sponsor, AI lead and champions. What time does each get? How is it recognised?
4. **Rituals** (15 min). Choose 3 rituals from the [operating model](/ai-native-teams/m11-change/operating-model/#rituals) and put them in the calendar now.
5. **Fears and friction** (20 min). Anonymously (sticky notes or a form), everyone writes their biggest concern about AI at work. Cluster them. For the top 3, agree a concrete response or commitment.
6. **Skills plan** (10 min). Which skills from [enablement](/ai-native-teams/m11-change/enablement/) need most work? How will you build them?

## Deliverable

A one-page **operating model**: roles and time, rituals and dates, top concerns with responses, skills plan. Add to the playbook.
