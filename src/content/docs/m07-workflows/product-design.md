## Product management

| Workflow | AI-native pattern | Human stays in charge of |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery & feedback synthesis** | Feed interviews, tickets, reviews and sales notes to AI; cluster themes with quotes and counts; track changes over time | Deciding which problems matter |
| **Specs & PRDs** | Draft from evidence; generate edge cases and acceptance criteria; critique as engineering, design and legal | The problem framing and trade-offs |
| **Prototyping** | Build clickable or working prototypes with AI app builders or coding agents *before* writing the spec; test with users the same week | What to test and what it means |
| **Competitive analysis** | Deep-research agents compile structured comparisons with sources | Verifying claims; strategy |
| **Roadmap communication** | Generate audience-specific versions (exec, sales, customers) from one source | Commitments |

The biggest shift: **prototypes are now cheaper than documents.** Showing users a working prototype often teaches more than a spec review.

## Design

- **Exploration:** generate many layout or concept variations quickly; use them to widen options, not to pick for you.
- **Design-to-code:** many design tools and coding agents can turn designs or screenshots into front-end code; designers can hand over working components, not just mock-ups.
- **Content and microcopy:** draft UI text in your voice from your content guidelines (an instructions file, [M3](/ai-native-teams/m03-context/instructions/)).
- **Accessibility checks:** use multimodal models to review screenshots for contrast, labels and reading order, then confirm with proper tools and real users.
- **Designing AI features:** show uncertainty, cite sources, make it easy to correct, undo and give feedback, and never hide that content is AI-generated.

## User research

- **Transcription and synthesis:** transcribe sessions, tag them against your research questions, and pull representative quotes, with links back to timestamps.
- **Guardrails:** get consent for recording and AI processing; remove personal data before using non-approved tools; **check every quote against the transcript**, as models can paraphrase quotes into things people didn't say.
- **Synthetic users** (AI personas): useful for rehearsing an interview guide or stress-testing a survey. **Not** a substitute for real users; treat any "finding" from them as a hypothesis.
