Speeding up individual tasks gives modest gains. The big gains come from **redesigning whole workflows**: removing hand-offs, changing who does what, and letting AI do the work in between. McKinsey's 2025 State of AI survey found that the redesign of workflows was among the factors most associated with organisations seeing value from AI ([The State of AI in 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2025)).

**Time:** ~3 hours · **For:** everyone

## Learning goals

- Use a repeatable method to find and redesign AI-native workflows.
- Know high-value AI patterns for each function.
- Build automations with no-code platforms and assistant features, with the right human checkpoints.
- Do trustworthy AI-assisted data analysis.
