## Customer support

- **Deflection with grounded answers:** a customer-facing assistant answers from your help centre with citations and hands off to a human with the full context ([M4](/ai-native-teams/m04-rag/)).
- **Agent assist:** suggested replies, relevant past tickets and knowledge articles for human agents.
- **Triage and routing:** classify urgency, product area and sentiment on arrival.
- **Knowledge loop:** cluster unanswered questions weekly → write or fix articles.
- **Checkpoints:** humans own refunds, exceptions, angry or vulnerable customers, and anything legal. Tell customers when they're talking to AI. In the EU, the AI Act's transparency obligations apply here ([M10](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/regulation/)).

## Sales

- **Account research and call prep** from public sources and your CRM.
- **Call summaries and CRM updates** generated from recordings (with consent).
- **Proposal and RFP drafts** grounded in an approved answer library.
- **Checkpoints:** pricing, commitments and anything contractual are human-approved. Personalised outreach at scale must follow consent and anti-spam rules.

## Marketing

- **Content operations:** briefs → drafts → variations per channel, using a brand voice instructions file.
- **Localisation** with human review by native speakers for important markets.
- **Campaign analysis** and audience research.
- **Checkpoints:** factual claims, comparisons with competitors, regulated claims, and AI-generated images of real people. Label synthetic media where required.

## Operations, finance and HR

- **Document processing:** extract structured data from invoices, contracts and forms with schema-constrained output ([M2](/ai-native-teams/m02-prompting/structured-output/)) and validation rules.
- **Reconciliation and anomaly explanation:** AI drafts the explanation; the numbers come from systems, not the model.
- **Policy Q&A** for employees, grounded in the handbook.
- **HR:** drafting job descriptions and onboarding plans is low-risk. **Screening, ranking or evaluating people is high-risk**: in the EU AI Act, employment uses are listed as high-risk ([M10](/ai-native-teams/m10-governance/regulation/)). Keep humans making the decisions, test for bias, and involve legal.

## Leadership and management

- **Meeting hygiene:** agendas from context, summaries with decisions and owners, follow-ups tracked.
- **Status on demand:** instead of weekly status reports, an assistant with access to the tracker and docs answers "where are we on X?".
- **Decision support:** pre-mortems, steel-manning alternatives, scenario analysis.
- **Writing for many audiences** from one source.
- **Checkpoints:** performance feedback and anything about individuals stays human-written and human-owned.
