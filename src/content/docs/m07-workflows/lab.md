<ul class="lab-meta"><li>⏱ 2 hours</li><li>👥 Cross-functional groups of 3–5</li><li>🧰 Whiteboard or online board, your approved AI and automation tools</li></ul>

## Goal

Produce a redesigned workflow, a working prototype of at least one AI step, and a pilot plan.

## Steps

1. **Choose the workflow** (10 min). Recurring, painful, measurable, and owned by people in the room. Examples: request intake → delivery, bug report → fix → customer update, monthly business review prep, new-hire onboarding.
2. **Map it** (25 min) using [step 1 of the method](/ai-native-teams/m07-workflows/redesign-method/#step-1--map-the-current-workflow). Mark wait times and hand-offs in red.
3. **Score and redesign** (30 min). Apply *eliminate → collapse → automate with checkpoints*. Draw the new workflow. Circle each human checkpoint and write why it's there.
4. **Prototype one AI step** (35 min). Build the most valuable AI step with real (non-sensitive) inputs, using a prompt-library entry, a custom assistant or an automation platform. Test on 5 examples.
5. **Plan the pilot** (20 min): metrics and baseline, who pilots, for how long, what would make you stop.

## Deliverable

A one-page **workflow redesign card**: before/after maps, checkpoints with reasons, prototype results, pilot plan. Add it to the playbook.
