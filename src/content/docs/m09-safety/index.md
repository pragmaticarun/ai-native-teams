AI introduces new kinds of risk: models can be manipulated by the content they read, agents can take real actions, and sensitive data can flow to places nobody intended. Every team member needs the basics; builders need the details.

**Time:** ~3 hours · **For:** everyone (lessons 1–2 and 4); builders and owners of agents (lesson 3 in depth)

## Learning goals

- Name the main security risks for LLM applications and agents, using the OWASP lists.
- Explain prompt injection and the "lethal trifecta", and why it can't be fully solved with prompts.
- Apply least privilege, sandboxing and approvals to agents and automations.
- Handle data safely: what can go into which tools.
- Address responsible-AI concerns: bias, transparency, IP, synthetic media and over-reliance.
