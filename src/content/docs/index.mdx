## What "AI-native" means here

An AI-native team doesn't just *use* AI tools. It **redesigns how work gets done** around them: AI is in the default path of everyday work, the team knows where it can and can't be trusted, quality is measured instead of assumed, and humans spend their time on judgment, taste and accountability.

## How the course is built

- **13 modules, ~55 lessons, 12 hands-on labs, a capstone and 12 self-check quizzes.** Each module ends with a lab your team runs together on real work.
- **Vendor-neutral.** Tools are named so you can find them, but every lesson teaches the pattern first. The [tools landscape](/ai-native-teams/reference/tools-landscape/) is dated and re-checked.
- **No invented numbers.** Claims about research, regulation or frameworks link to a primary source. Anything we could not verify is labelled.
- **Built for teams, not just individuals.** Every module has a "Team practice" section with rituals, artefacts and decisions to make together.
