<ul class="lab-meta"><li>⏱ 90 min</li><li>👥 Whole team, in pairs</li><li>🧰 Your approved assistant, a shared doc or wiki</li></ul>

## Goal

Leave with **8–12 tested prompts** for your team's most common recurring tasks, in a shared library format.

## Steps

1. **List recurring tasks** (10 min). Everyone writes tasks they do at least weekly that involve reading, writing, analysing or transforming information. Dot-vote the top 8–12.
2. **Pair up and draft** (20 min). Each pair takes 2 tasks and writes a prompt using the [five-part brief](/ai-native-teams/m02-prompting/anatomy/) and at least one [pattern](/ai-native-teams/m02-prompting/patterns/).
3. **Test on real inputs** (25 min). Run each prompt on 3 real inputs, including one awkward case. Iterate at least twice, changing one thing at a time. Note what each change did.
4. **Swap and stress-test** (15 min). Another pair uses your prompt *without your help*. If they can't, the placeholders or instructions aren't clear enough.
5. **Write the library entry** (10 min) with every field from the [library template](/ai-native-teams/m02-prompting/prompt-library/#a-team-prompt-library).
6. **Share one surprise** (10 min). Each pair: the single change that improved results most.

## Deliverable

A shared **prompt library** page with 8–12 entries, each with an owner and a "last tested" date.

## Stretch

Convert your two most-used prompts into a structured-output version (table or JSON) that could feed a spreadsheet or ticket system.
