Prompting is not about magic phrases. It's about **writing a clear brief** for a very capable collaborator who knows nothing about your situation. The skills transfer directly to delegating work to people.

**Time:** ~2.5 hours · **For:** everyone

## Learning goals

- Write prompts with the five parts of a good brief: role/situation, task, context, constraints, output format.
- Use the core patterns: examples (few-shot), decomposition, critique-and-revise, asking the model to ask you questions.
- Get reliable structured output (tables, JSON, checklists) that other tools can use.
- Iterate on prompts methodically instead of by trial and error.
- Build and maintain a shared team prompt library.
