Models know a lot about the world and nothing about your company. **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** fixes that by finding the relevant pieces of your knowledge and putting them in the model's context, with citations, at the moment of the question.

**Time:** ~3 hours · **For:** everyone for lessons 1 and 4; builders for lessons 2–3

## Learning goals

- Decide between RAG, long context, project files, fine-tuning and tools for a given knowledge problem.
- Explain the RAG pipeline: ingest, chunk, embed/index, retrieve (hybrid), rerank, generate with citations.
- Recognise state-of-the-art improvements: contextual chunking, hybrid search, reranking, query rewriting, agentic retrieval, permissions-aware retrieval.
- Evaluate a RAG system on retrieval quality *and* answer faithfulness.
- Build a working knowledge assistant over your team's documents.
